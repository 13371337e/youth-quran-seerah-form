# Youth Quran Seerah Deep Evaluation Form
## From Information to Transformation: A One-Year Intellectual and Practical Assessment

---

## FORM PURPOSE & INSTRUCTIONS

**This form is not a general religiosity test.** It evaluates what a participant actually learned during one year through:
- Professional education coursework
- 77 Makki (Meccan) chapters of the Quran
- Makki Seerah (Prophet's Meccan biography)
- Four months of spiritual training

**Core Question:** Did the learning remain as information (facts memorized), or did it progress to thought (understanding), insight (deeper perception), training (practical skills), and character (lasting transformation)?

## SECTION 3: QURANIC INTELLECTUAL DEVELOPMENT (77 Meccan Chapters)

**Purpose:** The 77 Meccan chapters weren't meant to memorize summaries. The Quran built first: certainty in God (Ma'rifat-e-Rabb) and belief in the afterlife (Aakhirat), then corrected worldly values, built character and purification, shaped the caller's personality, and gave responsibility for the community and humanity.

**This section assesses the progression through six stages:**

### RATING ITEMS (12 statements, scale 0-4)

| # | Roman Urdu | English Translation | Scale |
|---|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| 1 | ma'rifat-e-Rabb | Knowledge of God: I understand my ability, provision, success, and protection not as my effort alone, but as Allah's gift and trust | ☐ |
| 2 | Allah se taalluq | Connection with Allah: Prayer, Quran, remembrance, supplication, gratitude, and trust are practical support in my pressure, fear, and decisions | ☐ |
| 3 | aakhirat | Afterlife orientation: When immediate benefit competes with final outcome, I weigh the afterlife as the decisive factor | ☐ |
| 4 | ghalat meyaron ki islaah | Correcting false standards: Money, relationships, power, outward success, people's praise, and comfort no longer measure my worth or success | ☐ |
| 5 | tazkiya | Self-purification: I recognize my dominant desire, weakness, or wrong thinking and deliberately practice against it | ☐ |
