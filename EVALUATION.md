# One-Year Developmental Review of Quran, Seerah and Education

## What I Studied and What I Actually Became

- The Reality of Knowledge
- 77 Makki Surahs
- Makki Seerah
- Practical Transformation
- Four Months of Training

This form is **not a test of general religiousness**. It is an intellectual and practical review of what was studied during the past one year through **Professional Education, the 77 Makki Surahs and Makki Seerah**.

The objective is to see whether the learning remained merely **Information**, or whether it reached **Thought, Insight, Training and Character**.

---

## Principle for Filling the Form

The student should present the **actual state of the past one year**.

Only record a change for which there is an **example, habit, decision or observable evidence**.

### Rating

| Rating | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| **0** | The matter is not clear |
| **1** | There is information |
| **2** | Thought or priority has changed |
| **3** | Practice has begun |
| **4** | Consistent character and clear evidence |

The student's portion will be completed during the program.

The Naqeeb will later complete the assessment through an **individual discussion** and set only **two central objectives** for the next four months.

---

# Three Core Measures of the Review

| Measure | What will be observed? | What will be the evidence? |
|---|---|---|
| **Intellectual Soundness** | What was studied — was it understood correctly and in a connected manner? | A correct framework and example in one's own words |
| **Intellectual Insight** | Did the Quran and Seerah change the standard of success, priorities and responsibility? | Use of Quranic principles in real-life decisions |
| **Practical Consistency** | Is the change repeatedly visible in different circumstances? | A habit, action or responsibility from the past 30 days |

# Part One: The Reality of Knowledge and the Two Curricula

Professional Education gives me the **capability to do a certain kind of work**; the Quranic Curriculum determines:

- who I should become,
- why I should live,
- what I should consider success,
- what I should use my capabilities for, and
- ultimately, before whom I am accountable.

Together, the two curricula create a person who is:

- **Capable**
- **Trustworthy**
- **Responsible**

---

# Part Two: The 77 Makki Surahs and Stage-by-Stage Intellectual Development

The purpose of these Surahs was not to make us memorize **77 separate summaries**.

In the chronological journey of revelation, the Quran first developed:

1. Recognition of the **Rabb**
2. Conviction in the **Hereafter**
3. Rejection of the false standards of the world
4. Character and purification
5. The personality of the **Da'i**
6. Responsibility toward the **Ummah and humanity**

The review is of this same **stage-by-stage development**.

---

# Part Three: Makki Seerah and the Practical Roadmap of the Prophetic Revolution

Makki Seerah was taught not as a list of events, but as an **Operational Roadmap**:

- What was done?
- At which stage was it done?
- Why was it done?
- How was it done?
- What result emerged?
- What foundation did it create for the next stage?
